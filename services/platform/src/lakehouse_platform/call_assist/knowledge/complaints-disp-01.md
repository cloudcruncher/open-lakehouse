title: Chasing an existing complaint (DISP timelines)
tags: complaint_chase complaint chase deadline final response eight weeks ombudsman fos disp
---
1. Find the complaint and tell the customer its current status and who owns it.
2. Timelines (FCA DISP): payment services complaints get a final response within 15 business days (35 in exceptional cases). Other complaints get a final response within 8 weeks of receipt.
3. If the deadline has passed, or will pass before we can respond, tell the customer they can refer the complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service (FOS). They have 6 months from the date of our final response to do so.
4. Record the contact on the complaint and flag it to the complaint owner if it is within 5 days of its deadline.
5. Consider whether a goodwill gesture is appropriate for the delay. Don't promise an outcome of the investigation itself.
