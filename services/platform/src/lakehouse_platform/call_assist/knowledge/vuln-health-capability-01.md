id: VULN-HEALTH-01
title: Health or capability needs
tags: health capability vulnerability hospital diagnosis confusion understand carer
---
1. Adapt the call: speak plainly, check understanding, and offer to repeat things or send them in writing.
2. Offer adjustments, for example a named contact, a third-party mandate or power of attorney route, or a different contact channel.
3. If the customer seems confused, don't complete high-risk changes (new payees, large payments) on this call. Offer a follow-up.
4. With consent, record the needs so they don't have to be explained again.
