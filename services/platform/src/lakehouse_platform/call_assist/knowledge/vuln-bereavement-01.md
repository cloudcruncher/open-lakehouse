---
id: VULN-BEREAVEMENT-01
title: Supporting a bereaved customer
tags: bereavement vulnerability passed away died death funeral estate
---
1. Slow down. Say you're sorry for their loss, and let the customer lead the pace. Consumer Duty requires good outcomes for customers with characteristics of vulnerability.
2. Offer a transfer to the specialist Bereavement Support team, or a call back at a time that suits them.
3. Don't ask for documents on this call unless the customer wants to proceed. Explain what will be needed later (a death certificate, and the will or grant of probate).
4. With the customer's consent, record the vulnerability so they don't have to repeat themselves. Record only what is needed.
5. Joint accounts: payments can continue for the surviving holder. Don't freeze a joint account without a request.
