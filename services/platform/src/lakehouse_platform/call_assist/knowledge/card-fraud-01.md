---
id: CARD-FRAUD-01
title: Unrecognised card transaction or lost/stolen card
tags: card_fraud fraud stolen lost unrecognised payment card freeze dispute chargeback
---
1. Stop further loss first: freeze the card straight away. Don't wait until the identity check is finished if the caller reports theft in progress.
2. Go through the recent transactions with the customer and agree which ones they don't recognise. Pending card payments can often be stopped before they settle.
3. Raise a fraud claim for each disputed transaction. Under the Payment Services Regulations, unauthorised transactions are refunded by the end of the next business day unless there are reasonable grounds to suspect fraud by the customer.
4. Order a replacement card and confirm the delivery address on record. Never read the address out; ask the customer to confirm it.
5. Never ask for the full card number, PIN, CVV or online banking password.
