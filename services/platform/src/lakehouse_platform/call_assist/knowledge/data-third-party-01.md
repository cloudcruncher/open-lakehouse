---
id: DATA-3P-01
title: Requests about someone else's account
tags: third_party_request third party husband wife partner mandate power attorney disclosure
---
1. Don't disclose any information about another person's account, including whether it exists, unless the caller has a third-party mandate, power of attorney or other registered authority.
2. Explain kindly why, and describe how that authority can be set up. For a deceased customer, follow VULN-BEREAVEMENT-01.
