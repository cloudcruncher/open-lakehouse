---
id: IDV-01
title: Identity and verification before discussing an account
tags: identity verification idv security caller confirm date of birth
---
1. Before discussing any account details, verify the caller: full name plus postcode to find the record, then date of birth, then one more factor (for example the last 4 digits of the phone number on record).
2. Ask the caller for the information. Never read the details on record out to the caller.
3. If verification fails, do not discuss or change the account. Offer to call back on the number on record.
