id: PAYMENTS-FPS-01
title: Payment sent but not received
tags: payment_missing faster payment transfer arrived received missing trace
---
1. Find the payment in the recent transactions and check its status. Posted means it has left the account; pending means it is still being processed.
2. Faster Payments usually arrive within 2 hours, and by the end of the next business day at the latest. A payment held for a fraud check shows as pending.
3. If it was posted more than 1 business day ago, raise a payment trace with the receiving bank. Give the customer the reference and a timescale.
4. If the customer was pressured into making the payment or it now looks suspicious, treat it as possible authorised push payment (APP) fraud: follow CARD-FRAUD-01 and the APP reimbursement process.
