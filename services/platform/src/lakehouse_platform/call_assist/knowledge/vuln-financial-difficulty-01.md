title: Customer in financial difficulty
tags: financial_difficulty vulnerability struggling debt afford arrears
---
1. Acknowledge it without judgement. Customers often wait too long to ask for help.
2. Offer to pause collections activity and fees while options are explored (breathing space).
3. Signpost free, independent debt advice (for example MoneyHelper or StepChange). Offer a referral to the Financial Support team.
4. Don't offer new credit to resolve arrears on this call.
5. With consent, record the vulnerability and any agreed support.
